Echo the results the annotations can carry

The check-run annotations keep only part of the smoke log, and the part
they keep is shaped like the script's own "RUNTIME SMOKE:" output. Every
failure, the verdict and the three checks this pass was about (the /null tp
cannon, the doorway containment, the loot pickup) are now echoed once at
the end in that shape, so the numbers behind a pass are readable from
outside the runner.
