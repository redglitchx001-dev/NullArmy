Measure the barrage's height variety from the plan it fired

The pearls all fall at the same speed from the same sky, so their
positions a few ticks later say nothing about the heights they started
from - the first run caught both at y=-43 and read that as "one height".

The volley now remembers the heights and landings of the last ready plan,
and the cannon check asserts what the promise is about: at least two
different drop heights, landings at least 1.25 blocks apart, one planned
shot per live Null - while the real pearls still have to exist, be spent,
and put the bodies on the ground.
